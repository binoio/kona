Kona 2.2.0

Kona 2.2 lets you put your presets in the order you actually want them.

New

  • Drag to reorder presets — drag presets in the Kona Library to arrange them, and the order is honored everywhere: the menu bar dropdown, the Library sidebar, and the "Activate on launch" picker in Settings
  • Move Up / Move Down — reorder from the keyboard with ⌘⌥↑ and ⌘⌥↓, alongside Duplicate and Delete

"Indefinite Wake" stays pinned at the top of the list and isn't reorderable. Your preset order is saved and restored across launches. Requires macOS 14 or later.
